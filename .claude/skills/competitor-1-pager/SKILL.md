# Rules

- Every claim requires a dated source URL within the 7-day reporting window. If you cannot find one, write "No notable activity this period." for that competitor.
- Total report length: under ~600 words.
- Neutral analyst tone. Tide is the audience, not the subject. No marketing language.
- After writing the report file, print a single confirmation line and stop. Do not open or re-read the file.
